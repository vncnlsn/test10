Safety and HSE

APS personnel are accountable for health, safety, and environmental (HSE) compliance and accident prevention. Work follows the APS Safety Program Manual, the Injury and Illness Prevention Program (IIPP), and applicable client and project requirements.

In the field, APS personnel participate in daily safety briefings and job safety analyses (JSAs), monitor operations for compliance, and exercise stop-work authority when field conditions or activities fall outside established requirements.

APS Land Services is a member of ISNetworld.
