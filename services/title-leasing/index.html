Title and Leasing

APS researches surface and mineral ownership at any stage of a project, from early feasibility through active negotiation and acquisition. Preliminary research can reveal who controls the surface and minerals, how ownership is divided, where access may be difficult, and how many interests need to be addressed, before significant resources are committed.

Ownership records

Surface and mineral estates may have separated generations ago. Interests may be divided among multiple heirs, and federal, state, or tribal lands can involve separate records and approval requirements. APS traces and reconciles chains of title through county and parish courthouses, federal, state, and tribal land systems, and recorded instruments and historical archives, then maintains the information for use in GIS mapping, negotiations, acquisition, and field operations.

Services

  • Full title search to sovereignty
  • Surface and mineral ownership
  • Probate, heirship, and fractional interests
  • Title curative
  • Lease research, negotiation, and acquisition
