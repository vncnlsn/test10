Right-of-Way Acquisition

APS secures linear property rights for pipelines, electric transmission, utilities, telecommunications, roads, and other critical infrastructure. Work includes verifying corridor ownership and existing rights, identifying affected tracts and encumbrances, contacting landowners personally, negotiating compensation and property terms, and executing easement and access agreements.

Alignment changes

ROW acquisition develops alongside the route. A rejected agreement, divided interest, existing easement, utility conflict, or physical constraint can change an alignment after acquisition is underway, and a reroute can introduce new parcels, owners, title questions, mapping, and negotiations. APS identifies these conflicts early and keeps ownership, GIS, acquisition status, and route changes synchronized as the corridor develops.

Property rights

  • Permanent and temporary easements
  • Temporary and additional workspace
  • Ingress, egress, and access agreements
  • Property restrictions and restoration terms

Acquisition

  • Corridor ownership, title, and encumbrance review
  • Landowner negotiation and compensation
  • Tract acquisition tracking and reporting
  • Agreement execution and documentation
  • Agency, utility, and crossing coordination
