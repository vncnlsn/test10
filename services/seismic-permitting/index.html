Seismic Permitting

Seismic permitting has been at the core of APS's work for more than 45 years. APS manages land access for 2D, 3D, and 4D seismic surveys, microseismic, and VSP programs, and for CCUS, solar, data-center, and other complex field projects.

Landowner contact and conditions

Every property presents its own conditions. Access routes, crops and livestock, hunting seasons, fences, water wells, existing infrastructure, environmental restrictions, compensation, and potential damages can all affect where and how field operations are conducted.

APS contacts landowners personally to explain who APS represents, what the project involves, what access is being requested, and how the property will be used. Conditions are negotiated with the landowner, documented in the agreement, and carried into the field, where APS oversees compliance to protect all parties and the client's reputation.

Approvals and administration

APS coordinates applicable municipal, county, state, and federal approvals. Permit status is tracked and reported throughout the program. APS handles landowner payments, check processing, and required IRS filings, and coordinates surface damage settlement.

Early involvement

Preliminary ownership research can reveal who controls the surface and minerals, how ownership is divided, where access may be difficult, and how many interests need to be addressed, before significant resources are committed.
