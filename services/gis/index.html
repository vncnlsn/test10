GIS Services

APS builds project maps and databases that connect ownership and title information to the land. Surface and mineral ownership, leases, legal descriptions, topography, crops, infrastructure, project boundaries, and other relevant data are compiled and organized geographically for planning, negotiating use agreements, acquisition, and field use.

Data reconciliation

Surface and mineral interests do not always follow the same boundaries. Historical legal descriptions may not align with current parcels, and a change in project alignment can introduce a different set of affected properties. APS reconciles those differences across the project mapping and supporting data, keeping the information organized and current as the work develops.

Services

  • Comprehensive project mapping
  • Legal description and tract platting
  • Spatial data compilation and reconciliation
  • Project database development and management
  • Maps and exhibits
