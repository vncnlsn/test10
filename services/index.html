Services

APS provides four land services disciplines, engaged individually or in related scopes, from preliminary research and mapping through access negotiations, acquisition, and field oversight.

Engagement options

A single service

A seismic permitting program, a right-of-way scope, a title research assignment, or a mapping and database project can each be engaged on its own.

Related scopes

Ownership research, mapping, acquisition, and field work can be engaged together, with the information coordinated across disciplines.

Project review

For a project experiencing difficulties, consult with APS leadership to diagnose obstacles, restore control, and establish a clear path forward.
