About APS Land Services

APS Land Services provides land services for energy, exploration, and infrastructure projects.

APS has supported hundreds of energy and infrastructure projects across North America and conducted thousands of negotiations involving right-of-way, leasing, permitting, land access, and other property rights. Project experience spans difficult jurisdictions, challenging access, and demanding field conditions across private, state, federal, and tribal lands in 28 states, plus Alberta and Saskatchewan.

The company focuses on getting the details right and delivering on time, and takes pride in protecting landowners, field crews, the environment, and the projects entrusted to it.

Three locations: Utah, Montana, and Texas.

Leadership

Mark Fischbuch

President and Founder

Mark Fischbuch began working in the seismic industry in 1980, gaining experience in various positions throughout field operations. He built his own company, drawing on that background to improve the research, information gathering, and land access processes that too often delayed field work. Decades of ownership research, landowner negotiations, and oversight of field operations continue to shape how APS works.

Zane Nelson

Vice President

Zane Nelson joined APS in 2010 after distinguishing himself as a leader in geoscience business operations. He first served as CFO, transforming the company's financial structure, then took on project leadership and responsibility for day-to-day operations. As Vice President he oversees operations, works closely with clients and project teams, and continues to drive the company's growth.
