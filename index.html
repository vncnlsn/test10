What we do

Services

Seismic Permitting

Land access and permitting for 2D, 3D, and 4D seismic surveys, microseismic, and VSP programs, and for CCUS, solar, data-center, and other complex field projects. Seismic permitting has been the core of APS's work for more than 45 years.

Learn more →

Right-of-Way Acquisition

Permanent and temporary easements, workspace, and access agreements for pipelines, electric transmission, utilities, telecommunications, and roads, from corridor ownership and title review through tract-by-tract acquisition and agreement execution.

Learn more →

Title and Leasing

Surface and mineral ownership research at any stage of a project, including title searches to sovereignty, probate and heirship research, title curative, and lease negotiation and acquisition.

Learn more →

GIS Services

Project maps and databases connecting ownership and title information to the land, with legal description and tract platting, spatial data compilation and reconciliation, and project database development.

Learn more →

How we work

Project management

Senior leadership is engaged on every project. Personnel are vetted and experienced, and communication is handled directly by APS.

Title, GIS, acquisition, and field data are reconciled, with discrepancies tracked and resolved. Progress reports are clear and consistent.

Scope, specifications, and deliverables are defined upfront. Critical timelines are actively managed and consistently reported. Pricing is flexible: turnkey, unit, or project-based.
